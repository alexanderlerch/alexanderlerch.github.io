Now published in ACM Computing Surveys: [Survey on the Evaluation of Generative Systems in Music](https://doi.org/10.1145/3769106).

Despite the rapid progress in generative music models, evaluating these systems commonly referred to as music AI remains a major challenge. This comprehensive overview:
- Outlines key evaluation targets
- Compares methodologies across studies
- Discusses metrics and their limitations

The main goal of this text is to provide a clearer framework for assessing generative music systems — a crucial step toward more robust and meaningful research in this space. Writing this paper with Claire Arthur, Nick Bryan-Kinns, Corey Ford, Qianyi Sun, and Ashvala Vinay has been a long-term effort ideated at the ISMIR unconference in Milan 2023.

The [Music Informatics Group](https://musicinformatics.gatech.edu) is in the [Georgia Tech School of Music](https://music.gatech.edu), [Georgia Tech College of Design](https://design.gatech.edu).
